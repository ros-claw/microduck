# Relay Rumble (development, not accepted)

[中文方法](README.zh-CN.md)

This experiment replaces the cramped 3×3 single-point prototype with a 5×5 contact-consumed arena and five permanent outposts. Four fixed Chinese identities bind to distinct routing tactics: 张三 rushes, 二呆 avoids damage/crowds, 老六 attempts predicted interception, and 卷王 pursues the current beacon. Spawn directions rotate across seeds; identities retain their tactics. Interception intent alone is not evidence of successful blocking.

Exclusive, upright, actual loaded occupancy inside the beacon circle accumulates 0.75 seconds for one point. A rival inside the zone contests it even while airborne. Capturing moves the beacon through a public geometry-only station order and resets per-beacon progress. First to three points wins; timeouts do not award a forced winner. Fragile connecting tiles integrate filtered actual foot loads and fall under gravity after weld release. No root trajectory, external force, predetermined winner, new motor training, Jev call or native ROSClaw-agent execution is claimed for this mode.

The Chinese film replays one recorded match chronologically, with body-anchored names, colour badges, a full-arena minimap and event-aligned close-ups/slow motion. Original robot materials remain unchanged. Cameras and presentation marks do not affect physics.

See the Chinese method for exact run, capture, replay and 32-seed evaluation commands. Closed-loop replay checks exact states, controls, contacts and decisions against captured source/policy hashes. Results and limits are in [VALIDATION](VALIDATION.md). Generalization beyond this fixed map, hardware performance and independent viewer acceptance remain untested.

No release or tag may be created until the user explicitly confirms completion and approves publication. Existing source and local evidence are preserved.
